# Example data for latent variable models

Survey responses, school achievement, personality items, and sample
moments for latent variable analyses.

## Format

- `ESS_datLCA`:

  A data frame of 18,060 European Social Survey respondents, with eight
  political participation indicators, gender, age, and country.

- `hs_academic`:

  A data frame of 500 students with four binary school history
  indicators and achievement scores for grades 9 through 12.

- `icases`:

  A data frame of 28,143 respondents with 136 variables from the five
  HEXACO samples. The sample identifier is a character vector.

- `mtmm`:

  A list of sample sizes, means, standard deviations, correlations, and
  covariances for ABCD, HBN, NICHD Grade 6, and NICHD age 15.
